Hello 2020! Started the new year with an amazing trip to Busan with my friends. We explored the beautiful coastal city, taking in the stunning urban skyline and breathtaking ocean views.

From the bustling streets of Haeundae Beach to the colorful houses of Gamcheon Culture Village, every corner of Busan offered something special. We indulged in delicious local seafood, tried famous street food, and enjoyed the perfect blend of modern city life and natural beauty.

As we watched the sunrise over the ocean, we shared our hopes and resolutions for the upcoming year and our college journey ahead. It was the perfect way to welcome 2020 and strengthen our friendships before starting this new chapter of our lives. 🌊
