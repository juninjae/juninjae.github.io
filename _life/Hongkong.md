I went hiking on the Dragon's Back trail in Hong Kong, which offered breathtaking views of the city and coastline. The night view of Hong Kong's skyline was absolutely stunning, with countless skyscrapers lighting up the harbor.

Had an amazing experience at COA, Asia's #1 ranked cocktail bar, where I enjoyed their innovative and expertly crafted drinks. The atmosphere was sophisticated and memorable.

The food scene in Hong Kong was incredible - from traditional dim sum to street food in the night markets, every meal was a delicious adventure! 🍜
