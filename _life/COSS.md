I participated in the 2025 CO-SHOW MCU Competition finals held in Busan, a three-day event where teams worked on assigned tasks using microcontrollers. The competition evaluated circuit design, bare-metal programming, and MCU understanding.

Through effective teamwork and collaboration, our team achieved 3rd place and received the Busan Metropolitan City Mayor's Award.
